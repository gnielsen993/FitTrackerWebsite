# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users and purpose
People considering Stack for strength training. Gabe's confirmed goal is to sell the result of using the app, with features as proof. The primary action is downloading Stack; the secondary action is understanding why Pro is worth buying.

## Product truth
Free logging, routines, history, basic trends and muscle map. Optional Pro tools provide next-set suggestions, plate calculations, weekly target reviews, photo comparisons, guided max testing and more. Monthly, yearly and lifetime choices exist. The website does not invent prices or offer eligibility.

## Constraints
Static HTML, CSS and small vanilla JavaScript files. No framework or build dependency. Real app screenshots with their original proportions. The approved plate icon, established cream/charcoal identity and system fonts are retained. No invented testimonials, ratings or result guarantees.

## Data and accessibility
No account required to train. Optional sync and Health integration. Optional Firebase reports are on by default; do not claim zero analytics. Semantic HTML, keyboard access, reduced motion, readable contrast and mobile layouts are required.

## Release boundary
Prepared on a separate branch for 2.0. Not published or described as an already released update. Final production publication timing remains Gabe's decision.
