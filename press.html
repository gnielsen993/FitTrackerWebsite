Stack, at a glance.

Less tapping. More time lifting.

Stack helps lifters keep their routines ready, pick up from their last session and see their strength progress. Free workout logging is available on iPhone and iPad. Stack Pro adds next-set targets, plate calculations, Lock Screen logging and other optional training tools.

Short description

Stack is a gym workout log that helps you spend less time logging and get more use from your training history. Start free, then add Stack Pro for more direction between sets.
