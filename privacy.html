Privacy Policy

Your training record lives on your device. Optional services can sync it, connect it to Apple Health or help us improve Stack.

Prepared for Stack 2.0. Last updated: September 6, 2026.

This policy explains how Lauterstar Technologies, Inc. ("Lauterstar", "we" or "us") handles information in Stack and on this website.

The short version

  • No account is required to log workouts.
  • iCloud sync and Apple Health access are optional.
  • Usage analytics and crash reports use Firebase. Both are on by default and can be turned off in Profile → Privacy & Data.
  • Apple handles purchases. We do not receive your payment card details.
  • We do not sell your training data or use it for advertising.

What you save in Stack

Stack stores your workouts, routines, exercises, sets, weights, reps, notes, goals, bodyweight entries, progress check-ins, photos and settings on your device. We do not operate a server that stores your full workout log or progress-photo library.

Progress photos are stored in Stack's app storage. They are not automatically saved to your device's Photos library. You can choose to export or share information, including photos. Files you export are then handled by the location or service you choose.

Optional iCloud sync

If you enable sync using Sign in with Apple, supported training records sync through your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit service. Stack keeps the Apple user identifier needed for sign-in and sync. Apple handles this service under its own privacy policy.

Progress photos stored in Stack can also sync through your private iCloud account when sync is enabled. Older photos that have not yet been moved into the synced store may remain only on the original device. Stack also provides a separate photo backup.

You can sign out to stop sync. The app's account deletion flow attempts to remove synced data and lets you choose whether to erase local training data too. Copies in backups or files you shared are not removed by that action.

Optional Apple Health access

With your permission, Stack can write completed workouts to Apple Health. Its bodyweight integration can also read and write bodyweight entries. You choose which permissions to allow and can change them in your device's Health settings.

Health access is not required to log workouts. We do not use Apple Health data for advertising or sell it.

Optional usage analytics and crash reports

Stack uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to understand product use and diagnose problems. Usage Analytics and Crash Reports are separate switches in Profile → Privacy & Data. Both are on by default. Turning either off stops that collection in the app; when both are off, Stack does not start Firebase on the next launch. Turning a switch off does not automatically delete reports already sent.

Usage events include workout starts and completions, set counts, theme choices, imports and exports, feature use, setup milestones, upgrade-screen interactions, plan choices, purchase outcomes and whether Pro access was purchased, restored or otherwise activated.

Guided one-rep max events can include the named lift, test outcome, percentage change, step number and the original and suggested target weights when a target adjustment is shown. These are limited training-related values, not an upload of your full workout history.

Our custom analytics events do not include your free-text notes, routine names, progress photos, full workout records, receipts or payment card details. Firebase also processes app-instance or installation identifiers, app and device information, session information and other automatically collected service data. Analytics may include approximate location and purchase events generated by the SDK. These reports are not accurately described as anonymous or as containing no identifiers.

Crash reports can include crash traces, device and app details, and installation identifiers that help identify affected installations. Stack does not assign your name or Apple sign-in identifier as a Firebase user ID.

See Firebase's privacy information and Google Analytics data collection for details about the services. Apple may separately provide diagnostics when your device settings allow it.

Purchases and access to Pro

Apple processes monthly and yearly subscriptions and lifetime purchases. Stack receives verified purchase and entitlement information from Apple to determine access, restore purchases and handle subscription changes. Stack caches access on your device and shares the needed status with its own widget and Live Activity extensions.

We do not receive your payment card number or Apple Account password. If usage analytics are enabled, product and purchase events may also be reported as described above. For billing and purchase privacy, see Apple's privacy policy.

Support and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and investigate the issue. Please avoid including sensitive information unless it is needed for your request.

This website does not run an advertising or analytics script. Our hosting provider may process IP addresses, browser details and request logs to serve and protect the site. Links to Apple or other sites are handled under those services' policies.

Storage, retention and service providers

Your local records remain until you remove them or erase the app's data. Sync copies and exported backups follow the Apple or storage services you use. We keep support correspondence as needed to respond to requests and meet applicable obligations. Analytics and crash reports follow the retention settings and practices of the services described above.

Apple and Google may process information in countries other than where you live. Their privacy information explains their processing and safeguards. We may disclose information when legally required or to protect the safety and rights of users and our services.

Your choices and requests

You can export or delete training records, make a separate photo backup, turn off optional reporting, revoke Health permissions and stop sync. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or object to certain processing. Contact us to make a request. We may need information to verify it, and some requests are limited by law or by what we can identify in the records we hold.

Children

Stack is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information so we can investigate and take appropriate action. App Store age ratings describe app content and do not replace this policy.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Stack changes. We will update the date and provide notice of material changes through the app, website or release notes where appropriate.

Privacy questions: support@lauterstar.com.
